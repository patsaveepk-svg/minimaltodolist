The Chronology of Thai Currency

The journey begins in the shadows of prehistory, where the concept of trade was born through the exchange of natural tokens—beads, shells, and silver bars. The Coin Museum masterfully narrates how these primitive forms of exchange evolved into the iconic 'Pod Duang' or bullet coins of the Ayutthaya period.

Visitors are led through the pivotal transition during the reign of King Rama IV, as Thailand modernized its currency to align with international standards, introducing the first flat coins and the sophisticated minting technology of the Royal Thai Mint.

"History in your hands."
