ROYAL HERITAGE

Queen Sirikit Museum of Textiles

A sanctuary of Thai craftsmanship, preserving the intricate artistry of royal textiles within the historic walls of the Grand Palace.

THE ARCHIVE

The Ratsadakorn-bhibhathana Building

Commissioned by King Rama V in 1870, this historic edifice once served as the Customs House. Today, it stands as a state-of-the-art museum, harmonizing 19th-century architecture with contemporary conservation technology to safeguard Thailand's textile legacy.

The museum serves as a testament to Her Majesty Queen Sirikit's lifelong dedication to reviving the traditional weaving arts of Thailand, ensuring that these intricate skills are passed down through generations.
